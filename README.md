# Introduction-to-Arabic-_-Arabic-language-tributes
Basic facts about Arabic language
Arabic language has the following tributes:

1.	A Sematic language that consists of 28 letters, and it is written from Right to Left. 
2.	Diacritical Marks and Vocalization: each letter is annotated with a sign either above it or below it
3.	In Arabic there are 3 short vowels (signs), and 3 long vowels (letters) 
4.	Arabic is Phonemic Orthography language: This means that “you generally write what you hear, and you read what you see”. i.e. no silent letters are there. 
5.	There is no combination of letters to give specific voice like in English for example (Ghost, when, where …  Ch, ph, sh, -tia, -tion ..etc.)
6.	Arabic is a cursive language, i.e. the letters connect with each other to form the word
7.	Word morphology: You will create words from other words easily in Arabic, all words come from a Root word. It develops thinking and analytical skills. One root can generate dozens of words that are relevant to that root to use them when needed.
8.	Arabic is rich in vocabulary and precise expressions, and it will enrich your vocabulary in no time
9.	 The more you study Arabic the more you find that it strengthens your memory and enhances concentration skills due to the mental linking of words around the root it came from.
10.	Arabic language is a key to understanding Arab and Islamic culture.
